# WiggleWoo's Word Quest — Voiceover Recording Script

**Prepared for:** Voice Actor
**Date:** April 2026
**Game:** WiggleWoo's Word Quest (children's reading game, ages 4-8)

---

## Voice Direction

- **Tone:** Warm, encouraging, playful — like a friendly teacher or older sibling
- **Pace:** Slightly slower than normal conversation — kids need time to process
- **Energy:** Upbeat but not over-the-top. Celebrate effort, not just correctness
- **Word pronunciations:** Clear, natural, slightly segmented (e.g., "c-a-t... cat!"). Each word should be said once clearly
- **Format:** Individual WAV files per line, 44.1kHz, 16-bit minimum
