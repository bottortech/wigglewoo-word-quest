## Voice Direction

- **Tone:** Warm, encouraging, slightly playful — like a friendly older sibling. Never sarcastic, never patronizing.
- **Pace:** Slightly slower than normal conversation. Kids need processing time.
- **Energy:** Upbeat but not over-the-top. Celebrate effort, not just correctness.
- **Character:** WiggleWoo is a curious inventor character. Speak as a friend, not a teacher.
- **Word VOs:** Clear, single utterance per word. No slow segmentation — kids hear phonemes elsewhere.
- **Variants:** Where multiple variants are listed for one slug, vary delivery and word choice but keep the meaning identical. The game randomly picks one each time and never plays the same variant twice in a row.

## Delivery Format

- **Format:** WAV (44.1kHz, 16-bit min) → will be converted to .m4a
- **Volume:** Consistent across the session. Phrases play at 0.35 in-game, events at 0.4, words at 0.5.
- **Length:** Phrases under 2s, events under 3s, words under 1.5s.
- **Silence:** Trim leading/trailing silence to ≤100ms.
- **Naming:**
  - Pools (multiple variants): `slug-01.wav`, `slug-02.wav`, `slug-03.wav` …
  - One-shots: exact slug from the table (e.g. `crossmatch-intro.wav`)
  - Words: `word.wav` (lowercase, no spaces, e.g. `peg.wav`)
  - PictureMatch prompts: `pmatch-{vowel}-{n}.wav` (e.g. `pmatch-a-1.wav`)
  - Onboarding: `onboard-{descriptor}.wav` (e.g. `onboard-watch-cat.wav`)
